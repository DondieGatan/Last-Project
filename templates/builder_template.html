Resume Writing Tips for Any Career
I
Keep it concise

Aim for 1 page if you have less than 5 years of experience. 2 pages maximum for senior roles.

II
Use action verbs

Start bullet points with words like "Developed", "Managed", "Designed", "Implemented", "Achieved".

III
Quantify achievements

"Increased sales by 25%" is much stronger than "Helped increase sales".

IV
Tailor for each job

Customize your resume for each application. Mirror keywords from the job description.

V
Use simple formatting

Avoid tables, graphics, and fancy fonts. ATS systems prefer clean, single-column layouts.

VI
Proofread carefully

Spelling and grammar mistakes are among the most common reasons resumes get rejected. Have someone review yours.
